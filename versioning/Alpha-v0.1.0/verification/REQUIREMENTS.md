# Requirement delivery coverage — Minerva Blueprints Alpha-v0.1.0

[Português](REQUIREMENTS.pt-br.md) · [JSON](requirements/coverage.json) · [Catálogo](requirements/catalog.json) · [Migração](MIGRATION.pt-br.md) · [Release](RELEASE.md)

This matrix links the **50 applicable requirements**—30 for this model, 12 for integration and 8 for distribution—to delivered artifacts and assessment limits. The full catalog retains 80 requirements; the other 30 belong to the companion model. Source IDs and wording remain unchanged. Editorial adoption in this Alpha does not assert project-owner acceptance.

Facets identify the available kind of evidence. A delivered row is not a certificate for every application or an automatic proof that scenarios are sufficient. Actual executions are recorded in the edition report. Illustrative excerpts, C/Go adaptations and configured CI are not claimed as executed. Requirement titles and precise per-row limits below retain the source catalog's Portuguese; this translation does not rename its IDs.
