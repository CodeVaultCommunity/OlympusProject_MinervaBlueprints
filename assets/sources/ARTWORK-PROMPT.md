The overall canvas should remain navy, no large bright white areas. No logos, no text, no letters, no readable numbers, no title, no watermarks, no pseudo code, no screenshots. No owls or helmet because the supplied brand logo will be placed separately. Image is decorative only, not an actual technical diagram.
